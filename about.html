Who We Are

Born from a Passion for Exploration

Wanderlust Journeys was founded in 2011 by a group of seasoned travellers who believed that the best way to experience a destination was with an expert by your side. What began as a small boutique agency in London has grown into one of the world's most trusted names in luxury travel.

We don't just book trips — we craft transformative experiences. Every itinerary is built from scratch, drawing on 15 years of on-the-ground knowledge and a network of trusted local partners across 80+ countries.

From a couple's first adventure abroad to a solo expedition to the ends of the Earth, we believe every journey should be extraordinary. That belief is at the heart of everything we do.
